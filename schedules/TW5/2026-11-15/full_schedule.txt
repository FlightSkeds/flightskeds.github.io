TW5 USN CY26 CYCLE 2 PFA 10 WEEK NOTICE
This serves as the 10-Week Physical Fitness Assessment Cycle 2 2026 Notice for TW-5 USN students. All medically
cleared USN members are required to participate in all three fitness modalities (push-ups, forearm plank, cardio or
alternate cardio) to determine their PFA cycle overall score. A current PHA is required to participate in any portion
of the PFA. All PARFQs must be completed by 30 September. If you answer yes to any of the questions you are
required to print your PARFQ and get clearance from medical to participate and return the completed form to me
during BCAs.
USN personnel will have access to physical readiness information through the MyNavy portal and will need to
electronically submit their Physical Activity Risk Factor Questionnaire (PARFQ) via MyNavy portal. Please ensure
PARFQ and medical waivers are printed and readily available for the CFL. All BCAs will be conducted in the TW-5
HITU Conference Room (Rm and will be performed by the CFL and ACFL representatives on the dates indicated
below. All members must be in the official NAVY PTU, no exceptions. No courtesy BCAs will be performed during
the actual days of the BCA, but you can seek out the CFL or ACFL prior for a courtesy BCA.
All PRT participants must perform testing in the official NAVY PTU. “Bad day” PRTs will be discussed with the CFL on
a case-by-case basis. All retests must be completed within 7 days of a PRT Failure. Alternate cardio options for
retest (Bad Day) will be authorized on a case-by-case basis. Any questions or concerns, please contact me directly
and I will be happy to assist.
The PFA Cycle 2 2026 schedule is found below and will be posted in TW-5 STUCON spaces with sign-up sheets to
indicate which time slot/cardio option you plan to attend. All alternate cardio evolutions will be held at the NASWF
gym. All run evolutions will be held by the archery range on USS Intrepid Rd.

The intent is to have everyone complete prior to Thanksgiving to avoid conflicts with the holiday leave period. If
something comes up and you are unable to meet one of these dates, please contact me as soon as possible to
