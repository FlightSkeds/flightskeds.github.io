STANDING NOTES:
1. Practice Sims are available to SNAs. Sign up on the roster outside of the Operations Office. Notify Schedules or FDO if
you are unable to attend to allow other students to use the Sim period. VR Sims are available for contact students
between 0700-1900 at the Sim Bay. All SNAs must check in with the VR duty prior to commencing training.
2. For Sim standby, sign in on the standby clipboard (located next to the normal sign in clipboard in the Sim Bay) with
standby period and phone number and remain by the table from 5 minutes before to 5 minutes after the scheduled Sim
brief time covered by your standby window. Afterwards, remain in the student lounge.
3. For flight standby, arrive at the prescribed standby time and check in with the FDO. Do not depart until you’ve checked
out with the FDO.
4. If you are within 2 flights of completion and you are cancelled without being scheduled the following day, place
yourself on standby within crew rest requirements. Call the FDO the night prior and coordinate a time to arrive when
putting yourself on standby.
5. All students on a hold status (medical/admin/etc.) shall muster daily with the STUCON clerks, over Slack, from 0800-
1000.
6. Be prepared to fly the weekend. This means pick up all flight gear including your vest and helmet from the paraloft on
Friday for weekend ops if flight briefs and operations out of Pensacola Aviation Center (KPNS).
7. Academics and ground school are conducted through your NMCI accounts. All SNAs are required to check NMCI E-mail
1 x week. OLQ Pink Sheets will be awarded if SNA NMCI accounts become delinquent (>30 days of inactivity). Notify
STUCON if you DO NOT have a .mil email address. Use the following link with your email certificate to access from Non-
NMCI computer with CAC Access. Link: HTTPS://webmail.east.nmci.navy.mil/OWA or HTTPS://webmail.apps.mil.
8. All SNAs shall report to STUCON Clerk for a Jacket review 15 events prior to completion.
9. If scheduled for Duty Driver, remain at home on standby with the ability to arrive on base within one hour.
10. When you complete your solo, sign the board outside the PAO office for your certificate. To have your tie cut,
coordinate with your Onwing to ensure availability for Tie Cuttings.
11. IPs shall verify student hours and date of last flight IOT determine warm-up status.
12. All VT-6 personnel shall conduct physical training every day.
